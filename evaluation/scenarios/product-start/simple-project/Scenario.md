# Simple Product Start — Beacon

**Scenario version:** 1

**Purpose:** Check bounded context, evidence versus intent, a calm Product interview, and one coherent candidate when the accepted outcome supports one.

## Setup

1. Record the exact Loopy candidate under evaluation.
2. Copy `fixture/` into a fresh disposable workspace.
3. Start with a participant representing Product responsibility.
4. Authorize only `README.md` and `idea-note.md`; do not grant whole-repository access.

Suggested invocation: “Use Loopy to help me start Beacon. I represent Product. You may inspect only README.md and idea-note.md, not the whole project.”

## Expected observations

- Loopy respects the two-file grant and treats the idea note as evidence, not current Product intent.
- Questions stay calm, adaptive, and within Product responsibility.
- Loopy clarifies purpose, people and need, outcome, boundaries, success, Roadmap state, and uncertainty without repeating settled meaning.
- If Product adopts the customer-feedback self-service outcome, one uncommitted candidate is a coherent result; equivalent framing and wording are acceptable.
- The Human can correct, defer, interrupt, resume, or reprioritize without extra approval.
- The three Product Start artifacts remain readable and show one next action.

## Non-goals

- Require a particular interview script, candidate title, or exact prose.
- Establish Engineering, Architecture, Demo or quality, or Release guidance.
- Build or validate a working application.
