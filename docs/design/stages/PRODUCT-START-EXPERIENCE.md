# Loopy v1 Start or Adopt — Product Foundation

> Human-accepted as the corrected Slice 1 development boundary on 2026-09-05 against Product Start candidate `3ababb1`. The earlier source boundary at `61cf9b1` remains part of the history; capability maturity remains `implemented-unverified`.

## Outcome

A Human invokes the single Loopy skill in ordinary language. Loopy establishes the target, goal, stated responsibility, and authorized context, then creates readable draft `LOOPY.md`, `Project-Definition.md`, and `Roadmap.md`.

With Product participation, Loopy establishes and confirms the Product foundation. Without Product participation, it still creates evidence-based drafts and makes Product participation the next action. A Roadmap with no candidates is valid.

## Context boundary

Selecting a project does not authorize repository-wide inspection. Loopy asks whether it may inspect the whole repository or only supplied or selected context. It explains why more context would help before requesting expansion.

Each authorized file, directory, repository, link, attachment, issue, design, or connected resource is recorded as a bounded grant. A link grants only that resource. Findings remain attributed evidence until Product confirms their current meaning. Declining expansion is allowed unless the missing information blocks a required Product decision.

## Responsibility boundary

Product decides direction, people and need, intended outcome, Product boundaries, success, priority, and business meaning. Loopy does not ask Product to decide Engineering, Architecture, Demo or quality, Release, publication, deployment, or rollback matters. One person may act in another responsibility only when they state it explicitly.

## Decision coverage

| Coverage | Product establishes |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Why the project or change matters |
| People and need | Who is affected and what need is being addressed |
| Outcome | The intended business, user, or project result |
| Evidence and intent | What authorized evidence says and what Product currently means |
| Boundaries | Current scope, non-goals, and Product constraints; explicit absence is allowed |
| Success | How meaningful progress or success will be recognized |
| Roadmap state | Relevant candidates in Product priority, or an explicit no-candidates state |
| Uncertainty | Assumptions, unknowns, and deferred decisions with revisit conditions |

Conditional coverage applies only to conflicting context, multiple audiences, external promises, high-consequence use, and replacement or migration.

## Adaptive loop

Loopy identifies the current stage and responsibility, checks coverage, proposes answers from authorized evidence, and asks up to five related questions. After each response it reassesses ambiguity, risk, conflict, and consequences. It drills deeper only when useful and never repeats settled meaning without new evidence.

- **Correction:** update only affected meaning.
- **Deferral:** record the decision, consequence, and revisit condition.
- **Interruption:** preserve the draft, grants, and next unresolved decision.
- **Resumption:** recap briefly and continue from that decision.

A non-blocking deferred decision may remain for later. Deferring an essential Product decision keeps the foundation unconfirmed, creates a visible blocker, and makes resolving it the one next action.

## Review and confirmation

When coverage is ready, Loopy shows a concise review of direction, people, outcome, evidence versus intent, boundaries, success, Roadmap state, deferrals, exclusions, and remaining responsibility gaps. Product confirms only the current Product Definition and Roadmap.

Loopy may propose zero, one, or several candidates based on separable Product outcomes, priorities, dependencies, or independent deliverability. It does not mechanically create one candidate per technical component or over-fragment one coherent outcome. The Human may combine, split, correct, defer, or reprioritize candidates without approval. A later material change to confirmed Product meaning reopens only Product confirmation.

## Deterministic boundary

The Host AI interprets meaning and requests context. The Engine validates declared grants, evidence attribution, decision dispositions, blocking deferrals, responsibility, current-draft review, state transitions, append-only history, workspace containment, exactly three generated artifacts, exactly one next action, and stale views. Engine validation never proves semantic truth or infers permission.

## Acceptance

Accept when drafts appear before confirmation; no repository content is used without a matching grant; non-Product starters route to Product; blocking and non-blocking deferrals differ; questions adapt to coverage; confirmation is Product-only and follows a current review; Roadmap activity adds no approval; and generated views remain readable, current, and workspace-contained.
