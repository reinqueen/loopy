# Milestone Demonstrate and Accept

Use this internal route when a Human asks to see, operate, record, review, accept, correct, or defer an exact Milestone Build Candidate that is ready for Demonstration. Never expose this route as a separate skill or command.

Load the current Definition, Plan, Proof, exact candidate, and synchronized project views. Apply the shared after-response move-selection loop. Present the requested observable outcome and important limitations before implementation detail. Demonstration does not replace independent Proof and never mutates the product.

Choose the method just in time from the observable surface and available environment. Reuse confirmed Demo guidance when it applies. A human-guided Demo lets the Human operate or directly observe while Loopy guides. An AI-operated recorded Demo preserves exact actions, observations, outputs, media, environment, and limitations. Use both only when each adds useful understanding. Do not hard-code brands or claim unavailable tools.

Before operating, establish or reuse exact authority for the candidate, source/workspace snapshots, environment, expected operated target, tools, actions, scope, external systems and effects, accessibility needs, participant, and perspective/context. Reuse it only while every consequential dimension remains unchanged. Ask once for changed authority. Never infer product mutation, Release, push, merge, tag, deployment, publication, or distribution authority.

Verify the operated target before recording an observation: establish that the endpoint, process, file, service, or other observable target actually represents the authorized exact candidate in the expected Demo environment, and preserve independently inspectable verification evidence. A matching source or workspace hash alone does not establish what a browser, process, or external tool reached. On a mismatch, record no candidate observation. Treat it as a Demo environment/target failure and retry, reauthorize changed target/environment authority, or block. Route to Build only when candidate-bound product evidence—not a tool or target collision—supports an implementation defect.

Persist meaningful canonical observations without presenting the artifact after every turn. Publish the required digestible `Demo.md` when it guides a live Demo, on explicit request, at useful pause/block, when ready for judgment, and at final disposition. Its opening summary immediately states the requested outcome, what was demonstrated, result, important limitations or unproven behavior, and the human decision or one next action. Keep raw commands, logs, media, hashes, and technical detail linked below that summary. Put local evidence inside the project workspace and submit its workspace-relative path; the Engine verifies its hash and renders a link relative to the nested `Demo.md`. Use durable HTTPS references only when independently resolvable. Mark session-only references ephemeral and inaccessible; they cannot support demonstrated coverage.

Before asking for judgment, reflect each committed acceptance criterion as demonstrated, failed, deferred, inaccessible, or unproven. The Host AI owns semantic sufficiency and missing-perspective judgment; the Engine validates only formal coverage and provenance. Explain a materially absent perspective and ask for judgment or recommend participation without mechanically requiring a title or Product/Engineering label.

Only genuine explicit human judgment may accept. Record the human and the perspective/context they brought. Keep three provenances distinct: genuine human input, simulated participant input, and proxy AI review. Never translate a simulated or proxy response into `actorKind: human` or `personalJudgment: true`, even when it uses first-person language. Record simulated/proxy input only as advisory provenance; it remains outside the **Human judgment** section and cannot complete the Milestone. Clear natural-language intent from an actual human is enough; do not demand a magic phrase or another generic approval.

Acceptance binds the exact demonstrated candidate and committed boundary and makes the Milestone Complete but unreleased. Correction returns to Build when meaning and approach remain current, Planning when the delivery/evaluation approach changes, or Change Management when accepted meaning changes. Preserve failed and superseded Demo history. Defer, pause, or block with one next action. Candidate or anchor drift invalidates the Demo. Stop before Release or any external authority not expressly granted.
